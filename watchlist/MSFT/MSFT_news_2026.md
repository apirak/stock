## 2026-04-27 — OpenAI Partnership Amended: Exclusivity Ends, Revenue-Share Capped at $38B Through 2030
- **Source:** CNBC / Forbes / OpenAI blog
- **URL:** https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/27/openai-microsoft-partnership-revenue-cap.html
- **Category:** M&A / Partnership / Industry
- **Summary:** Microsoft and OpenAI restructured their partnership: OpenAI's revenue-share payments to Microsoft capped at $38B through 2030 (at the 20% rate), cloud exclusivity ended (OpenAI free to run on other clouds), and Microsoft secured IP rights to OpenAI models until 2032. Microsoft retains its ~27% OpenAI stake (~$135B as of the Oct 2025 recapitalization).
- **Impact:** Removes a key bear case (unbounded revenue-share outflow) but also removes Azure's exclusive claim on frontier OpenAI workloads — a two-sided restructure.
- **Time Horizon:** Long
- **Thesis Impact:** Mixed — clarifies economics; modestly weakens Azure exclusivity moat.
- **Intrinsic Value Impact:** Roughly neutral (caps outflow; opens competitive cloud risk).
- **Why It Matters:** The OpenAI relationship is Microsoft's single largest AI strategic dependency; this amendment defines its economics for the rest of the decade.
