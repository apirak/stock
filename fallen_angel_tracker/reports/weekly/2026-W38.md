## ส่วนที่ 1 — Fallen Angels ประจำสัปดาห์

### #1 NIKE, Inc. (NKE)

**Verdict:** Pass - Temporary · **แผน:** Accumulate 1st Tranche · **เพดานพอร์ต:** 6.0% · **ราคา:** $37.05 vs FV $49.35 (ส่วนลด 24.92%)

**📈 ดูกราฟ:** [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/chart/?symbol=NKE) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/NKE) · [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/nke/)

**🏰 ทำไมป้อมปราการยังแข็งแกร่ง:** This is the cleanest fallen angel on the board: the 25% discount comes with a balance sheet that is effectively net cash (Net Debt/EBITDA 0.41x, interest expense negligible), ROIC 17.9% vs WACC 8.7%, and — the single most important datum — gross margin holding at ~43% (42.9% now vs 43.5-44.6% historically). Stable gross margin through a -31% six-month drawdown is textbook evidence of pricing power intact: the brand still commands its premium while management resets inventory and wholesale relationships. The market is extrapolating turnaround duration into permanent brand damage; nothing in the numbers shows customers stopping choosing the swoosh. First tranche is justified; respect the plan, not the emotion.

**📉 ตลาดกำลัง overreact อย่างไร:** Downgrades on tariff/oil/rate cost noise (Baird cut Nike, adidas, Dick's) and a self-inflicted wholesale/DTC strategy reset have the stock at a historic low (return_6m -30.7%). These are cyclical cost pressures and fixable strategy errors, not loss of the reason athletes and retailers choose Nike.

**🎯 แผนแบ่งไม้เข้าซื้อ:** Tranche 1: 25-30% of the intended full position at current levels (discount ~25%, clean balance sheet). Tranche 2: 30% after the next earnings confirms gross margin holding above ~42% and inventory normalization on track. Tranche 3: remainder only when revenue returns to growth, keeping total position within the 6% portfolio cap.

**🚪 จุดยอมแพ้ (Invalidation Criteria) — เจอเมื่อไหร่ต้องตัด:**
- Gross margin breaks below 40% for 2 consecutive quarters (pricing power eroding)
- Market-share loss to adidas/On/Hoka persists 3+ quarters with no innovation-cycle response
- ROIC falls below WACC (8.7%)
- Inventory liquidation/discounting deepens rather than normalizes over the next 2 quarters
- Net Debt/EBITDA rises above 1.5x (balance sheet no longer pristine)

### #2 PayPal Holdings, Inc. (PYPL)

**Verdict:** Watch · **แผน:** Wait for Next Earnings · **เพดานพอร์ต:** 5.0% · **ราคา:** $54.03 vs FV $57.07 (ส่วนลด 5.33%)

**📈 ดูกราฟ:** [TradingView](https://www.tradingview.com/chart/?symbol=PYPL) · [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/quote/PYPL) · [StockAnalysis](https://stockanalysis.com/stocks/pypl/)

**🏰 ทำไมป้อมปราการยังแข็งแกร่ง:** Quantitatively this is the strongest business on today's list: ROIC 26.8% vs WACC 9.4%, FCF yield 12.0%, net cash (net debt/EBITDA -0.07x), coverage 14.5x, and margins rising four years straight. The two-sided moat of PayPal/Venmo (consumer + merchant network effects, stored credentials) remains real. The framework's problem is entry price: a 5.3% discount is no compensation for an execution-dependent turnaround of the growth engine. Let the transformation show up in numbers or the price fall to a real discount.

**📉 ตลาดกำลัง overreact อย่างไร:** No margin-of-safety: at a 5.3% discount the price does not compensate for the open strategic question of branded-checkout share loss. News flow is active but ambiguous — a new CEO rejected a $50B buyout to pursue a Venmo/agentic-payments transformation, a genuine reinvention risk regardless of quality.

**🎯 แผนแบ่งไม้เข้าซื้อ:** No entry today. Tranche 1 (25-30%) if the discount widens past 15% (~$48.5 on today's fair value) with branded checkout volumes at least flat; Tranche 2 (30%) when transaction margin dollar growth re-accelerates; Tranche 3 (remainder) only after two quarters of evidence the agentic-payments/merchant initiatives are additive, not defensive.

**🚪 จุดยอมแพ้ (Invalidation Criteria) — เจอเมื่อไหร่ต้องตัด:**
- Branded checkout TPV growth turns negative for 2 consecutive quarters
- Operating margin reverses its 4-year improvement trend
- Major merchant partners publicly dropping PayPal as a checkout option
- FCF yield falls below 6% (buybacks/transformation burning the cash moat)

## ส่วนที่ 3 — บทเรียนการลงทุนสไตล์ Buffett

### 📚 Meta in 2022 — Capex Panic and the -75% Drawdown

Meta fell from ~$380 to ~$90 on Reality Labs spending and an Apple ATT headwind. Bears called it structural (TikTok, privacy); it turned out the ad network's network effects and targeting data were intact, and cost discipline ('year of efficiency') released enormous margin. Lesson: even genuine structural questions can be over-discounted when the core cash engine still compounds; size tranches, don't catch with one hand.
