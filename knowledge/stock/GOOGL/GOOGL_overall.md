## Business Model
Organized in three divisions (FACT — FMP profile): **Google Services** — Search advertising, YouTube (ads + subscriptions), Android, Chrome, Google Play (apps, in-app purchases, digital content), hardware (Pixel, Nest, Fitbit), Google Maps/Gmail/Drive/Photos; this is the profit engine and majority of revenue (ESTIMATE ~75-80%). **Google Cloud** — GCP infrastructure, Workspace collaboration suite, Vertex AI; the growth engine, now selling TPU systems as well (FACT — Q2 2026 coverage). **Other Bets** — health tech, internet services (Waymo, Verily, etc.); small, loss-making optionality. Customers: advertisers (self-serve and enterprise), consumers (free products monetized via ads and subscriptions), and enterprise cloud/AI customers.

## Revenue Drivers
1. **Google Services** — majority of revenue and operating profit; Search dominant (FACT, general)
2. **Google Cloud** — fastest growth: Q1 2026 +63% to $20.0B; Q2 2026 +82% to $24.8B (~$100B annualized run-rate), aided by TPU system sales revenue recognition (FACT — Yahoo Finance / Mirror Review / EarningsLens)
3. **AI capex supercycle** — FY2026 capex guidance raised to $195-205B, roughly 2.2x FY2025's $91.4B (FACT — Q2 2026 coverage)
4. **YouTube** — ads + subscription bundling; durable engagement moat (FACT, general)
5. **Gemini Enterprise** — cited at ~90% of Fortune 100 adoption (CEO-stated in Q2 2026 release, FACT as quoted)
6. **Monetization of equity stakes** — ~$99B unrealized equity gains (SpaceX + one private company) in Q2 2026 reported net income (FACT — EarningsLens)

## Industry Structure
Three overlapping arenas. **Digital advertising:** large but cyclical with macro; GOOGL + META dominate; retail media and AI-answer ad formats shifting the mix. **Cloud:** secular growth, AI infrastructure demand exploding; GOOGL is #3 behind AWS and Azure (FACT, general) but growing fastest (+82% Q2 2026). **AI:** model + silicon + distribution race; economics of inference still settling. Regulation is heavy and structural: US v. Google remedies proceedings (search distribution deals), EU DMA and antitrust fines, app-store rules (FACT, general ongoing). Disruption risk: AI answer engines (ChatGPT, Perplexity) disintermediating the traditional search interface (OPINION).

## Competitive Position
Search: dominant global share (ESTIMATE >85-90% of query volume) reinforced by Android/Chrome default distribution. YouTube: #1 global video platform. Android: #1 mobile OS by units. Cloud: #3 share but #1 growth rate among hyperscalers, differentiating on custom TPU silicon + AI stack. AI: one of only two-three companies with full vertical integration — own silicon (TPU), own frontier models (Gemini), own distribution (Search/YouTube/Android/Chrome/Workspace) (FACT products exist; advantage magnitude OPINION). Cost edge in inference from TPU is a genuine differentiator vs NVIDIA-dependent rivals (OPINION).

## Moat: **Strong**
Why: (1) Search — habit + default distribution + two decades of index/data compounding (FACT); (2) YouTube two-sided network effects (creators ↔ viewers) — no credible substitute at scale (FACT/OPINION); (3) Android/Chrome distribution reaching billions of devices (FACT); (4) Switching costs in Workspace and GCP (FACT, general); (5) Scale economics in AI — ability to fund $195-205B/yr capex from internal cash while staying profitable (FACT — guidance vs FY2025 OCF $164.7B, ESTIMATE funding mix). Caveat: the search moat is strongest at the distribution/data layer; a consumer shift to AI answer engines could slowly erode query volume even if Google supplies the models (OPINION). Moat is Strong but the interface layer is under genuine attack for the first time in two decades (OPINION).

## Management
Sundar Pichai — CEO since 2015, engineer-led, presided over the AI-first pivot and the Cloud turnaround from distant #3 to AI-infra leader (FACT/background). Capital allocation: first-ever dividend + enlarged buyback initiated 2024 (background); FY2025 buybacks $45.7B — slowed vs $62.2B FY2024 as AI capex ramped (FACT). The $195-205B FY2026 capex guide is one of the largest single-year investment programs in corporate history — aggressive, conviction-driven, and a real ROI bet (FACT guide; OPINION framing). Governance: founders control via super-voting Class B; minority Class A holders have limited influence (FACT structure). Key-man risk moderate (bench is deep — DeepMind, Cloud leadership) (OPINION).

## Major Risks
- **Business:** AI answer engines reducing search volume/click-through (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini itself cannibalizing); ad-market cyclicality; AI capex ROI uncertainty — $195-205B/yr must produce adequate incremental revenue
- **Financial:** FCF currently flat-to-suppressed while capex doubles; if revenue growth disappoints while capex is committed, multi-year FCF squeeze (FACT pattern, ESTIMATE risk)
- **Competitive:** AWS/Azure in cloud (share fight at the #3 spot); OpenAI/Microsoft in consumer AI; Meta in ads; NVIDIA-dependent rivals vs TPU cost curve
- **Regulatory:** US v. Google remedies (search default/distribution — ongoing, general); EU DMA/fines; app-store payment rules; potential AI regulation (FACT, general)
- **Technology:** model architecture shifts; inference-cost deflation helping rivals; content-moderation/deepfake costs on YouTube
- **Management:** key-man moderate; founder-class governance limits minority influence
- **Valuation:** at $337.12 (~31x trailing FY2025 EPS) the stock discounts substantial AI success; disappointment compresses the multiple sharply (see status file)

## Long-Term Investment Thesis (3–5 reasons)
1. Search + YouTube + Android form the world's most valuable intent-and-attention network, generating the cash that funds everything else (FACT basis, OPINION framing)
2. Google Cloud is now a hypergrowth AI business (+82% Q2 2026, ~$100B run-rate) differentiating on custom TPU silicon — the #3 hyperscaler is growing fastest (FACT)
3. Full vertical AI stack — TPU, Gemini, Vertex, and unmatched consumer/enterprise distribution — gives cost and speed advantages most rivals cannot match (FACT products; advantage magnitude OPINION)
4. Fortress economics: 32.8% net margin, 21.8% ROIC, OCF $164.7B and rising, funding $200B capex AND buybacks AND a dividend simultaneously (FACT)
5. Per-share compounding: share count down ~7% in three years while EPS compounds ~30%+ — even if growth decelerates, per-share value keeps compounding (FACT trend)

## Thesis-Breaking Conditions (observable)
- Search revenue declining for 2+ consecutive years as AI answers displace queries, with no offsetting monetization
- Cloud growth decelerating below ~25% while AWS/Azure sustain higher growth (share loss), or Cloud failing to reach material operating margin by 2028
- Capex discipline failing: OCF growth < capex growth for 3+ consecutive years, or FY2027 capex guided above $220B without matching revenue acceleration (returns not materializing)
- Major antitrust remedy forcing default-search/distribution changes that cut Search revenue >10%
- Gross margin structurally below ~52% (AI inference costs overwhelming the ad model)
- Forced breakup/divestiture of core properties with value-destructive terms

## Relevant Valuation Methods
- **DCF (primary):** fits — huge, real cash flows; but near-term FCF is deliberately depressed by the AI capex cycle, so scenarios must span capex-ROI outcomes with an explicit capex-normalization path; ranges, not point values
- **Earnings-power / exit P/E (secondary):** fits — apply conservative exit multiple (16-20x) on FY2029-30 EPS; primary cross-check to DCF
- **Sum-of-the-parts (tertiary):** useful — market clearly prices Google Services and Google Cloud as distinct assets; Services on ad-earnings multiple, Cloud on revenue multiple, plus net cash and Other Bets optionality
- **EV/EBITDA, PEG, FCF-yield sanity checks** vs mega-cap peers (AMZN, MSFT, META, AAPL)
- NOT suitable: DDM (token ~0.26% yield), P/B, asset-based
